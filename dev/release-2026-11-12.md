# Release: Debt Snowball v4.6, 12/11/2026

- What changed for people using it: a "not financial advice" line under the top bar; the top bar reads v4.6.

## Known, not fixed in v4.6

- Loose ends on the shared-plan offer when saves are failing at the moment Yes is pressed.
- On a weak signal the artifact copy shows the examples and "Not saving" rather than the browser copy.
- "Delete all my debts" can be undone by another tab or device that still holds the plan.
- Not yet tested on the real artifact with two accounts.
