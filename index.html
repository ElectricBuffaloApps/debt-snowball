        Credit use

        How much of each limit you're using. It's one of the things that affects your credit score.

        What Experian says: try to keep the share of your limits you use below 25% to help protect your credit score. It counts credit cards and overdrafts, not loans or mortgages, and looks at each card as well as your total. Your credit file can take around 4 to 8 weeks to catch up after you pay balances down.

        A paid-off card still adds its limit to your total while the account stays open. Source: Experian's guide to credit utilisation.

              Your data

              These are estimates. Interest is worked out monthly from each APR and minimum payments stay fixed (that's how a snowball works). Each 0% deal gets an even share of what's left on it every month so it clears by its end date, and switches to its new rate in the month it ends. On a card with more than one balance, payments go to any instalment plan payment first, then the highest-rate balance. Check your real balances each month and update them here.
